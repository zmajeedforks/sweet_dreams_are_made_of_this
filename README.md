# Sweet Dreams (Are Made of This)


## Frame 1

<div align=center>
<figure data-timestamp=00:00 data-lyrics="(Doom drum beat)">
  <img src=images/000752_fist_pound_start.png width=100%>
  <figcaption>Frame 1: Dave's fist pounds the boardroom table</figcaption>
</figure>
</div>
<br>

The video begins as an anonymous fist pounds a black table to the first doom beat. A streak of light down the middle illuminates the bare hand. It feels like a call to order at the start of important proceedings, even like a judge banging the gavel to start a trial.

Later we'll see the fist is Dave's.

## Frame 2

<div align=center>
<figure data-timestamp=00:03 data-lyrics="">
  <img src=images/003603_annie_frozen_baton.png width=100%>
  <figcaption>Frame 2: Annie in frozen conductor pose, holds baton in front of glowing white screen</figcaption>
</figure>
</div>
<br>

The camera pulls out to show a boardroom table with conference chairs and notepads all around. The darkened red walls are covered with framed gold records. The room feels part of exclusive environs that showcases imposing achievements and commands hushed respect.

At the far end of the table Annie stands frozen facing right. We'll call her Boardroom Annie to distinguish her from other personas of Annie.

She has a baton or wand in her raised left hand. A bright screen behind her creates a silhouette of her that looks like a conductor just before she cues the orchestra to start playing. It heightens anticipation for what could happen next.

## Frame 3

<div align=center>
<figure data-timestamp=00:04 data-lyrics="">
  <img src=images/004424_annie_bent_baton.png width=100%>
  <figcaption>Frame 3: Annie bangs baton down onto boardroom table in puppet-like movement</figcaption>
</figure>
</div>
<br>

What happens next is unexpected. Annie suddenly turns right and bends down to strike the baton on the table on the second doom beat.

What's surprising about this scene is the way she moves. Her movement is abrupt and doesn't look natural. It seems controlled like a marionette that slumps when its strings are loosened and suddenly stops when its strings are pulled.

The image of Annie's frozen silhouette a moment ago is now very reminiscent of the start of a shdow puppet play.

These puppetlike movements of Annie at the beginning are important to keep in mind among later imagery of Annie in poses of authority.

## Frame 4

<div align=center>
<figure data-timestamp=00:08 data-lyrics="">
  <img src=images/007690_annie_rocket_screen.png width=100%>
  <figcaption>Frame 4: A rocket blasting off into space appears on screen behind Annie as she looks straight into camera</figcaption>
</figure>
</div>
<br>

Annie slowly raises her head, again in rather controlled fashion, to look straight at the viewer.

We clearly see what she's wearing for the first time. It's a black business suit with a grey tie on a crisp white dress shirt. We also get a clear view of her face. The closecropped shocking orange hair. The penetrating eyes lined in smoldering black. The heavily rouged sharp cheekbones tapering to bright red lips.

An image appears for the first time on the screen behind her. It's a rocket blasting off into space.

Annie begins singing the first verse, "Sweet dreams are made of this". The rocket fades to a view of Earth in space.

## Frame 5

<div align=center>
<figure data-timestamp=00:10 data-lyrics="Sweet dreams are made of this">
  <img src=images/011466_annie_point_earth_sweet_dreams.png width=100%>
  <figcaption>Frame 5: Annie points baton at Earth on screen behind her</figcaption>
</figure>
</div>
<br>

Annie stands up straight and points the baton back toward the screen behind her.

She completes the verse, "Sweet dreams are made of this", saying "this" just as she points the baton at Earth. It's natural to feel confused and wonder how Earth is what sweet dreams are made of. Like WTF?! Or How on earth is ...?!

It's only with the help of scenes that follow that we can piece "this" together.

## Frame 6

<div align=center>
<figure data-timestamp=00:12 data-lyrics="Who am I to disagree">
  <img src=images/014368_annie_astronauts_whoami.png width=100%>
  <figcaption>Frame 6: Astronauts inside rocketship appear on screen behind Annie</figcaption>
</figure>
</div>
<br>

The screen changes to show astronauts inside the rocketship. Presumably they're now in space far above Earth.

Annie sings, "Who am I to disagree".

Now at least we see other people. This is good because there are no dreams if there's no people.

Are sweet dreams those of astronauts dreaming to reach space above and beyond Earth? More abstractly, are sweet dreams the highest aspirations of humans? Maybe it's the ambition to reach the highest heights, literally out of this world looking down at existence.

Does it mean the ingredients and recipe for sweet dreams is in what it took for humans to conquer space? But just what is that exactly? Scientific and technological excellence? Political will and determination of society?

It's still not clear what "this" is. But whatever it is, the suggestion is it got highly trained specialists like astronauts into space. And who can disagree with their recipe for success.

## Frame 7

<div align=center>
<figure data-timestamp=00:20 data-lyrics="Everybody's looking for something">
  <img src=images/020947_annie_crowds_everybody.png width=100%>
  <figcaption>Frame 7: Dave looks at computer monitor and types on keyboard as crowds appear on screen behind Annie</figcaption>
</figure>
</div>
<br>

After Annie spins a globe on the table and sings, "I travel the world and the seven seas", we get the first shot of Dave sitting at the other end of the table.

He's typing away at a computer keyboard while looking at a small cube monitor in front of him. The monitor displays an image of Annie and he holding hands.

What's Dave doing here? Is he like a court reporter or a clerk taking meeting minutes, recording the proceedings? Or does he have more active control over what's taking place in the room?

Annie sings "Everybody's looking for something" as the screen behind her shows a crowd of businesspeople all walking in the same direction. The large crowd is "everybody". They're walking with aim and purpose, "looking for something".

But why people in business dress though? Why show them walking to work? Why are they all moving in unison in the same direction?

There's a strong suggestion of corporate herd mentality, of robotic controlled behavior driven by goals set by an unseen authority.

## Frame 8

<div align=center>
<figure data-timestamp=00:23 data-lyrics="Some of them want to use you">
  <img src=images/025870_annie_point_dave_some_use_you.png width=100%>
  <figcaption>Frame 8: Annie accusatively points baton at Dave while his monitor shows the two of them holding hands in lotus pose</figcaption>
</figure>
</div>
<br>

Annie thrusts her baton toward Dave and sings "Some of them want to use you". Dave is looking at an image of Annie and himself on his monitor.

The monitor shows them with eyes closed holding hands in the same boardroom with gold records on the wall behind them. It's slightly jarring to see a very different image of them in the same room on the monitor. Is the monitor showing something that took place already? But it's a view we haven't seen before. Is it something that will happen in the future? Or is it a completely imagined view?

This is all rather intriguing. It leaves the lyric ambiguous. Is Annie accusing Dave or warning him when she points the baton at him? Which half of the verse "Some of them want to use you" is about Dave? Is he "you", one who is used? Or is he one of "them", one who uses people?

The next scene reinforces the sense Annie is simply playing out a script. The previous full scene of Annie is transferred to the monitor. Dave continue to type on the keyboard while Annie sings, "Some of them want to be abused".

Transitioning the full scene of Annie in her most domineering pose to the small monitor box atop Dave's computer effectively conveys how powerless she truly is.

It's now inarguable that Dave is the puppetmaster. And Annie is willingly being programmed to sing and act as he chooses.

<div align=center>
<figure data-timestamp=00:46 data-lyrics="Ooh">

After the quick fade to the face of Meditating Annie, we see a big change in the boardroom.

Meditating Annie and Dave sit crosslegged side by side in lotus pose on the boardroom table. Their eyes are closed. Their arms are raised above their knees and their hands are clasped in the middle.

The mounted gold records on the wall are prominently visible behind them.

This is the same image that appeared earlier on Dave's monitor.

There is an incongruity to their meditating while surrounded by symbols of big business. It could imply a rejection of materialistic goals and values in favor of spiritual peace.


## Frame 15

<div align=center>
<figure data-timestamp=00:48 data-lyrics="Aah">
  <img src=images/048454_annie_dave_lotus_waves_ooh.png width=100%>

</figure>
</div>
<br>

The video has a dark, dystopian tone from the beginning with suggestions of technological mind control. But it turns really weird at this point.

Distorted grey waves begin to emanate from Annie and Dave's bodies as they sit meditating on the boardroom table.

It looks like a transformation is taking place inside Meditating Annie and Dave. And it's spreading out into the boardroom and possibly beyond.

The waves resemble the channels or trails in Donnie Darko that come out of people and represent their paths in the future. The parallel or alternate dreamworlds in the movie also bear some similarities to the video. Even though Donnie Darko is set in the mid-1980's, it was released in 2001, over 18 years after the Sweet Dreams video.

## Frame 29

<div align=center>
<figure data-timestamp=01:25 data-lyrics="Hold your head up">
  <img src=images/085312_cow_eye_boardroom_hold_head_up.png width=100%>
  <figcaption>Frame 29: Closeup of head of black cow in boardroom as its big eye looks at viewer</figcaption>
</figure>
</div>
<br>

This is our first glimpse of the cow. It's an extreme closeup of its head centered on its big wide open eye looking at the viewer. The head is black and angled. There's a patch of white on its forehead.

It could pass for the head of a horse at first glance.

The lyric "Hold your head up" plays.

There are several reasons this image is very unsettling. And takes the surreal aspect of the video up a notch.

One reason is anyone staring back at you feels uncomfortable. It forces you to acknowledge their presence. It makes you question their intentions. It makes you feel vulnerable.

It's doubly uncomfortable if an animal stares at you. All the uncertainties and your anxieties go up a level because people are not used to animals examining humans this way.

Also there are some wellknown images that are similar but very violent. One is the cow eye scene created by Luis Bunuel and Salvador Dali in the film Un Chien Andalou. Another is the horse head scene in The Godfather. Both scenes are rather infamous and had left their mark on popular culture by the 1980s.

Lastly the cow is shown in the boardroom with the screen, gold records and conference chairs in the background. It's one of the last places we'd expect to see an animal.
